Business logic

Business rules

  1. One user has one profile and can own many lectures.
  2. One lecture has one transcript and one AI analysis.
  3. A lecture can have current topics and predicted future topics.
  4. Each quiz question has answer options and one correct answer.
  5. A quiz attempt belongs to one user and one lecture.
  6. Teacher tasks and AI practice tasks are stored separately by type.
  7. A task can have many reminders.
  8. Deleting a lecture also deletes its dependent learning data.
  9. Row Level Security lets a student read only their own rows.
